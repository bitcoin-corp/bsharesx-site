How it works

Three layers, one truth.

Nothing about who owns what changes hands on our servers. The company runs its own register; bSharesX keeps the on-chain record and the holder's view in step with it.

The register

The register of members is the legal title. The company keeps it, as company law requires, and its directors approve every entry.

The token

An on-chain record that mirrors the register, entry for entry. It's evidence of the holding, not the title: when the register changes, the record is reissued to match. If the two ever differ, the register wins.

The certificate

A live view in the holder's wallet that redraws when the register changes. Want a keepsake? Mint a snapshot of it.

Registers are run by the companies themselves. bSharesX is the software they keep them with. It suits any company, from three friends with a shop to a firm with hundreds of members. Our own company is the first register in testing.
